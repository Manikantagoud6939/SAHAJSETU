Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

SAHAJSETU TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED ("SahajSetu", "we", "our", or "us") operates the SahajSetu mobile application and web ecosystem, encompassing Local Shop AI, FarmLink Marketplace, and FoodShare Network. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact Information: Name, phone number, WhatsApp contact details, business/store name, farmer organization (FPO) registration, or NGO verification credentials.
  • Location Information: Regional city, district, and optional geolocation for hyper-local store stock matching, farm dispatch routing, and emergency food rescue courier navigation.
  • Voice Data (Local Shop AI): Short audio input captured exclusively during active voice billing sessions to perform speech-to-text itemization in vernacular dialects. Audio samples are processed ephemerally and not sold to third parties.
  • Transactional & Order Data: Items billed, wholesale procurement quantities, surplus food volumes, and transaction ledger timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To power vernacular voice billing, automated itemization, and digital receipts.
  • To match farmer produce harvests directly with local retail store demand without intermediate arbitrage.
  • To coordinate urgent surplus food pickup and delivery to registered orphanages and shelters.
  • To provide customer support, dispute resolution, and regulatory compliance (tax and invoice reporting).

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared strictly with:

  • Verified logistical couriers (volunteer or fleet) to fulfill delivery or pickup requests.
  • Escrow payment gateway partners for direct settlement upon receipt verification.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when strictly required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 at rest) to safeguard user records. Account holders can request complete erasure of their data and transactional logs at any time.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users may request the review, update, or deletion of their account and associated data by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@sahajsetu.internal.
