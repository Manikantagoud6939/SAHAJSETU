Product 02 • Agri-Supply Disintermediation

FarmLink Marketplace: Connecting Growers Directly to Retailers

Traditional produce journeys pass through 4 to 7 intermediaries, causing massive post-harvest loss and draining farmer profitability. FarmLink Marketplace creates a direct digital highway connecting Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and smallholders directly with kirana stores, restaurants, and institutional kitchens.

30-40% Better Realization

Growers retain maximum share of consumer rupees without unrecorded commission deductions.

18-Hour Harvest-to-Shelf

Aggregated cold logistics reduce transit decay from 28% down to under 3%.

Smart Quality Grading

Computer vision grading at village aggregation points assigns objective quality tiers.

Instant Escrow Payouts

Direct UPI bank transfers upon digital gate receipt, eliminating months-long credit delays.

Product 03 • Zero-Waste Social Mission

FoodShare Network: Solving Hunger Through Zero Food Waste

While millions face nutrition insecurity, tons of fresh perishables and prepared food get discarded daily due to demand mismatches. FoodShare Network is our civic logistics platform connecting retail shops, catering hubs, and corporate cafeterias with verified food banks and shelters.

1-Tap Surplus Dispatch

Whenever a local store or restaurant has surplus edible stock or safe prepared meals, a single button flags nearby NGO volunteers.

Food Safety & Shelf-Life Protocol

Strict safety checklists, temperature monitoring guidelines, and digital liability waivers ensure food reaches recipients hygienically.

Tax & ESG Impact Credits

Donating businesses receive automated ESG carbon-offset documentation and Section 80G tax benefit ledgers.
